Trauma and urgent neurosurgery

Head injury, bleeding and hydrocephalus

Our surgeons provide emergency neurosurgical care at Mississauga Hospital and see patients in clinic for follow-up after urgent conditions.

  • Traumatic brain injury or concussion needing neurosurgical review
  • Chronic subdural hematoma and other intracranial bleeding
  • Hydrocephalus and shunt malfunction
  • Spinal fractures and follow-up after spinal cord injury

In an emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department.
