About Dr. Merali

Dr. Zamir Merali is a general and endovascular neurosurgeon at Trillium Health Partners in Mississauga, caring for patients from across Peel Region and the Greater Toronto Area.

He completed his medical degree and a six-year residency in neurosurgery at the University of Toronto, where he gained broad operative experience in spine and tumour surgery. His general neurosurgical practice includes degenerative, traumatic and tumour-related conditions of the cervical, thoracic and lumbar spine, as well as brain tumours, with cancer care coordinated through the Carlo Fidani Regional Cancer Centre.

He then completed a fellowship in endovascular neurosurgery at St. Michael’s Hospital in Toronto, focused on minimally invasive, catheter-based treatment of brain aneurysms, vascular malformations and other cerebrovascular disease.

He is a staff neurosurgeon at Trillium Health Partners and its Physician Lead for Neurointerventional Radiology. He is also an Assistant Professor at the University of Toronto and the Toronto Metropolitan University School of Medicine.

Clinical interests

General neurosurgery

  • Degenerative spine disease, including cervical myelopathy, radiculopathy and lumbar spinal stenosis
  • Spinal fractures
  • Brain and spinal tumours
  • Neurotrauma and emergency neurosurgery

Endovascular neurosurgery

  • Brain aneurysms
  • Arteriovenous malformations and dural arteriovenous fistulas
  • Pulsatile tinnitus and idiopathic intracranial hypertension
