About Dr. Merali

Dr. Zamir Merali is a neurosurgeon at Trillium Health Partners in Mississauga, caring for patients from across Peel Region and the Greater Toronto Area.

Dr. Merali completed his medical degree and a six-year residency in neurosurgery at the University of Toronto. He then completed a fellowship in endovascular neurosurgery at St. Michael’s Hospital in Toronto, focused on minimally invasive treatment of cerebrovascular disease.

He is a staff neurosurgeon at Trillium Health Partners and its Physician Lead for Neurointerventional Radiology. He is also an Assistant Professor at the University of Toronto and the Toronto Metropolitan University School of Medicine.

Clinical interests

  • Brain aneurysms, arteriovenous malformations and dural fistulas
  • Pulsatile tinnitus and idiopathic intracranial hypertension
  • Brain tumours
  • Degenerative spine disease, including myelopathy and radiculopathy
  • Neurotrauma and emergency neurosurgery
