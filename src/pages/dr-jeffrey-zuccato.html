About Dr. Zuccato

Dr. Jeffrey Zuccato is a neurosurgeon with advanced training in skull base surgery and neuro-oncology. His clinical practice focuses on the surgical treatment of brain and skull base tumours, using both open and minimally invasive endoscopic approaches.

He earned his medical degree at the University of Toronto, where he also completed his residency in neurosurgery. He then completed a fellowship in skull base neurosurgery and neuro-oncology at the University of Oklahoma College of Medicine, and went on to serve as an Assistant Professor in its Department of Neurological Surgery before joining Trillium Neurosurgery Clinic.

Dr. Zuccato also holds a PhD in Medical Science from the University of Toronto, where he studied new molecular biomarkers for brain tumours. His research focuses on diagnostic and prognostic brain tumour biomarkers, liquid biopsy and non-invasive biomarkers, and bringing molecular biomarkers into patient care. He has published in journals including Nature Medicine and Neuro-Oncology, and has presented his work at major national neurosurgery and neuro-oncology meetings.
