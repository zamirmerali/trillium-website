What to include

  • Imaging reports, and where the imaging was done so we can view the images
  • Whether you think the patient needs Rapid Access assessment

Pulsatile tinnitus

Some patients with pulsatile tinnitus have a treatable vascular cause, such as venous sinus stenosis or a dural arteriovenous fistula. A brief referral with a CT or MR venogram is enough for us to assess whether a patient is a candidate for treatment. More about pulsatile tinnitus.

Emergencies

Patients with acute neurological deterioration should be sent to the nearest emergency department. For urgent physician-to-physician consultation, call CritiCall Ontario at 1-800-668-4357.

Rapid Access Clinic

Expedited assessment for patients who need to be seen quickly but don’t need the emergency department or admission.
