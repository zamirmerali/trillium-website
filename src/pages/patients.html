Patient information

Your visit, step by step

What to expect when you are referred to our clinic, what to bring to your appointment and answers to common questions.

  1. Referral

    All appointments require a referral from a physician or nurse practitioner.

  2. We call you

    Once your referral has been reviewed, our office calls you to book.

  3. Consultation

    You meet your surgeon at our clinic at 101 Queensway West to go over your imaging and options.

  4. Treatment

    If you have surgery, it takes place at Mississauga Hospital, with follow-up at our clinic.

Before your appointment

What to bring

  • Your Ontario health card
  • A list of your current medications, including any blood thinners
  • Imaging discs or reports from outside Trillium Health Partners
  • A list of your symptoms and the questions you want to ask
  • A family member or friend, if you would like support

If you need to cancel or reschedule, please call (905) 291-4506 as early as possible.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a referral to see a neurosurgeon?

Yes. All appointments require a referral from a physician or nurse practitioner, such as your family doctor, a walk-in clinic physician, an emergency physician or a specialist. Ask them to fax the referral to 1 (905) 228-4111.

Is my appointment covered by OHIP?

Consultations and medically necessary surgery are covered for patients with a valid Ontario health card. Please bring your health card to every visit.

How long will I wait for an appointment?

It depends on how urgent your condition is. Every referral is triaged by the surgical team, and patients who need to be seen quickly may be booked through our Rapid Access Neurosurgery Clinic.

How will I know when my appointment is?

Our office will call you. Please make sure your referring clinician has your current phone number, and answer calls from unknown numbers while you are waiting.

Does seeing a neurosurgeon mean I need surgery?

No. Many of the patients we see do not need an operation. Your surgeon will review your imaging and symptoms with you and discuss all of the options, including treatment without surgery.

Where will my surgery take place?

Surgery is performed at Mississauga Hospital, Trillium Health Partners, 100 Queensway West, across the street from our clinic.

What should I do if my symptoms get worse while I wait?

If you develop a sudden severe headache, new weakness, difficulty controlling your bladder or bowels, a seizure or confusion, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department. For other concerns, call our office at (905) 291-4506 or speak with your family doctor.
